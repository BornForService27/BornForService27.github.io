About BFS

WELCOME TO BORN FOR SERVICE

Born For Service School of Ministry is a Christian ministry training community and mentorship programmes committed to biblical learning, spiritual formation, character development, and preparation for effective service.

Our desire is to help believers grow in their understanding of Scripture, develop their God-given abilities, and become better equipped to serve God and His people.

LEARN MORE ABOUT BFS

Our Vision

To raise and equip believers who are biblically grounded, spiritually mature, character-driven, and prepared for effective service in the Kingdom of God.

Our Mission

To provide biblical and practical ministry training that helps believers grow in knowledge, character, spiritual maturity, leadership, and effectiveness in Christian service.

    How studying and graduating at BFS works

    Course access

    Students access their courses through the BFS WhatsApp group.

    Assessment

    Each course has its required assignments, quizzes, or examinations.

    Progress tracking

    BFS records completion and assessment results for every course.

    Graduation eligibility

    Students must complete and pass all the courses required for the programme. The minimum passing mark is 50%.

    Certificate issuance

    Once an authorised BFS administrator verifies eligibility, the final Certificate or Advanced Certificate is issued.

    No partial awards

    Completing only some of the required courses does not qualify a student for the final award.

    How admission works

    1. Read about the courses taught at BFS.
    2. Complete the online application form. Choose the Certificate (1 year) or the Advanced Certificate (2 years). Give a valid WhatsApp number and attach a copy of your NRC (or your passport if you are not Zambian).
    3. BFS reviews your application. Submitting an application does not mean you have been admitted.
    4. BFS contacts you on WhatsApp with the decision.
    5. If you are accepted, BFS issues your admission letter with your student number and the programme you are admitted to, and your WhatsApp number is added to the BFS WhatsApp group, where lessons, assignments and announcements are shared.
    6. Every admitted student must also have a BFS Identity Card (K100) and a BFS T-shirt (K200). These are not tuition fees.
    7. The graduation fee can be paid in instalments, but it must be paid in full 2 months before graduation.

    How to pay for the Identity Card and T-shirt

    1. Send the amount by Airtel Money to the official BFS number, +260 975 153 242 (Identity Card K100, T-shirt K200).
    2. Forward your Airtel Money receipt to the same number on WhatsApp: +260 975 153 242.
    3. BFS records your payment and sends you a receipt by email.

    Payment is made after your application is accepted. BFS will ask you on WhatsApp for your T-shirt size (S, M, L, XL or XXL) and a passport-style photo for your Identity Card.
